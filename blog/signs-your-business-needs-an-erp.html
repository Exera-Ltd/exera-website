5 signs your Mauritian business has outgrown spreadsheets

Spreadsheets are brilliant until they aren't. If a few of these sound familiar, it's probably time to move to a proper system.

EX By Exera Ltd·30 October 2026·5 min read

Almost every business in Mauritius starts the same way: a few spreadsheets, a WhatsApp group, maybe an off-the-shelf invoicing app. It works, right up until it doesn't. The tricky part is spotting the moment you've outgrown it, usually a while after you actually did. Here are five signs.

1. The same number lives in five different places

Stock on a spreadsheet, sales in an app, customer details in your phone, invoices in a folder. When one thing changes, you update it in several places, and sooner or later they disagree. If you've ever argued with your own data about how much stock you really have, that's sign number one.

2. Month-end takes days, not minutes

If closing the books, pulling sales figures or checking what each product line actually earned means an evening of copy-paste, you're paying for software with your time. A real system gives you those numbers on demand, not after a reconciliation marathon.

3. Nobody can work unless they're at that one computer

The "master file" lives on one laptop, and only one person can edit it at a time. That doesn't scale past a couple of people, and it's a disaster the day that laptop dies. Cloud-based systems let your team work at the same time, from anywhere, with daily backups so a lost device is an inconvenience, not a catastrophe.

4. You're about to deal with MRA e-invoicing by hand

Mauritius is moving towards electronic invoicing through the MRA. Handling that on spreadsheets is painful and error-prone. An MRA E-Invoicing compliant system turns compliance into a setting rather than a scramble, which matters more every year.

5. Growth makes things worse, not better

This is the big one. With the right system, more customers and more products make your business stronger. On spreadsheets, every new product line, outlet or staff member adds friction. If growth feels like it's breaking your admin instead of rewarding it, your tools are holding you back.

What to look for in an ERP in Mauritius

If a few of those rang true, here's what actually matters when you choose a system, beyond the feature checklist:

  • Fits your workflow: software should adapt to how you work, not force you to change everything to suit it.
  • Fair, predictable pricing: no per-seat surprises or hidden fees, so you can budget with confidence.
  • Local, reachable support: a team that understands Mauritian business and answers quickly (ideally on WhatsApp).
  • MRA compliance built in: so e-invoicing is handled, not homework.
  • Room to grow: unlimited records and the option to add custom features as you scale.
