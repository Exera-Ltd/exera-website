Our software runs on secure, encrypted, energy-efficient infrastructure. We apply enterprise-grade security measures and regular backups to protect data against loss or unauthorised access.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
